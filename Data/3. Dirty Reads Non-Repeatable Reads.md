## 🔴 1. Dirty Read

### ✅ What is it?

A **dirty read** happens when **you read data that hasn’t been committed yet** by another transaction.

That data might later be **rolled back**, which means you saw something that **never really existed**.

---

### 📦 Example (Bank Transfer)

- **Transaction A**: Starts transferring $100 from Account A to Account B.
    
    - It **subtracts** $100 from A, but hasn’t **added** it to B or **committed** yet.
        
- **Transaction B**: At the same time, reads Account A’s balance.
    
    - It sees the **updated value**, even though the transaction isn't committed.
        
- Later, **Transaction A fails**, and the transfer is **rolled back**.
    
- But **Transaction B used that wrong data**.
    

🧠 Dirty read = You saw **"dirty" (unconfirmed)** data.

---

## 🟠 2. Non-Repeatable Read

### ✅ What is it?

A **non-repeatable read** happens when **you read the same row twice** during a transaction, and **get different data** because another transaction **changed it** in between.

---

### 📦 Example (Reading Product Price)

- **Transaction A**: Reads product price = $50.
    
- **Transaction B**: Updates product price to $60 and commits.
    
- **Transaction A**: Reads the same product again — now it’s $60.
    

You got **different results** for the **same row** in one transaction.

🧠 Non-repeatable read = The data **changed while you were looking**.
